# Product direction

The user delegated visual direction for a contest-focused workflow product. The audience is operations teams and judges evaluating whether a workflow runs from intake through a human decision.

- Identity: an operations workbench that puts evidence, routing, and the approval handoff in view.
- Mood: direct, assured, practical, and specific to operational work.
- Palette: warm paper `#F4F0E7` and deep pine `#19352F` establish a printed-work-order feel; muted clay `#B9543D` marks the main decision/action. Neutral ink and paper tints support reading. The clay accent is reserved for primary actions and active workflow state.
- Typography: a readable system sans for forms and data, paired with a restrained editorial serif for the landing headline to distinguish the product from a generic developer dashboard.
- Motif: a connected approval trail, representing the actual path from request to checks to a named human decision. It is data-bearing, not a decorative background grid.
- Composition: the landing page opens with the real workflow interface, then explains the flagship flow, then lists the additional templates. The signed-in app centers the pending-review decision rather than fabricated metrics.
- Motion: brief focus and state transitions only, to acknowledge interaction without slowing a work queue.
- Dials: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1.

No customer logos, testimonials, portraits, performance figures, or compliance claims are approved. Use a text wordmark and clearly labeled sample records only.
